How the impersonation heuristic uses this

For each new pool's token, the MetadataSimilarityHeuristic embeds "Token SYMBOL (Name). Newly deployed at 0x..." via OpenAI, runs a top-3 cosine search against this corpus, and produces a high-risk score when:

  • the top match has cosine similarity > 0.92, AND
  • the new token's deployer EOA does NOT match the canonical deployer of the matched corpus entry

That is the canonical impersonation pattern: someone deploys a token named "USDC2" or "uSDC" from a wallet that has nothing to do with Circle.
